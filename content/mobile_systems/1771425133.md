Positioning system that exploits a stochastic model of the environment to compute position avoiding the signal measurement error, the approach is based on [scene analysis](/1771425122#scene-analysis) approach

One of the improvements over [RADAR](/1771425160) is the rail tracking principle that computes position based on the most recent calculated one and by following allowed paths (*e.g. no wallk trough walls*)

![](/1794065316.png)

In the ekahau solution nodes ask to be located at the central server
