# 16-11-2026

So this is my first persona game (*actually is my first Atlus game*), after 15 years of playing square enix RPG's, it feels like a breath of known but fresh air, There are a lot of JRPG's standard mechanics, but also some cool extensions from the dating sym and visual novels worlds.

One of the things that I really like is the way the game represents the Japan society, it shows this extreme selfish and individualistic people and builds the classic hero story around this, it also touches some really complex topics like student harassment, work abuse and mafia.

> [!DEV]
> - stunning visuals
> - the mix between normal life and phantom thieves gameplay surprisingly fun
> - cool time dynamics
> - love the parallelism between psychology and palaces
